I'm a data scientist who looks for opportunity in data and an entrepreneur who carries those insights into the real world — from funnel and retention analysis at a fintech startup to KPI frameworks for AI deployments in a smart-factory division.

**Most recently at LG CNS**, I was an AI Engineer on the Smart Factory Division's New Business Development task force in Seoul, defining the KPIs and success criteria that decided which AI proofs of concept moved to deployment, and analyzing production and construction data for manufacturing and energy clients. Before that I built growth and retention analytics at a fintech startup, a RAG assistant for Bay Area Rapid Transit, and the data stack of my own startup. Now I'm completing an **M.S. in Applied Data Science at the University of Chicago** (expected Dec 2027), after a B.A. in Data Science and a minor in Computer Science at UC Berkeley.
